🏗️ System Architecture

🧠
LSTM Regime Detector Neural network identifies bullish market conditions with 64.3% accuracy. Uses 14-day lookback with 20 technical indicators (RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, etc.).
📊
Entry Filtering 40% confidence threshold + regime detection ensures only high-probability setups are traded. Prevents overtrading in choppy markets.
🛑
Risk Management 2% per-trade limit, 6% portfolio stop-loss, 1.75× ATR stops. Position sizing scales dynamically with volatility.
🎯
Take Profit Strategy 2R take profit (twice the risk), 48-hour position expiry. Locks in gains while preserving capital.
⏱️
Position Management Maximum 48-hour hold time + auto-flatten on time exit. Prevents overnight slippage and market gap risk.
📈
Backtesting Framework Complete trade logging, capital curve tracking, and statistical analysis. Forward tested on completely unseen 2024+ data.

🔄 How It Works

  1. Market Data: System ingests 1-hour OHLCV Bitcoin data from Bitstamp
  2. Feature Engineering: Calculates 20+ technical indicators (RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, ATR, EMA, SMA, etc.)
  3. Bull Detection: LSTM model predicts bullish regimes with 64.3% accuracy
  4. Entry Signals: When confidence > 40% + regime = bullish, generates trade signal
  5. Position Entry: Opens position with 1.75× ATR stop-loss
  6. Exit Management: Takes profit at 2R or exits after 48 hours (whichever comes first)
  7. Risk Control: Caps per-trade loss at 2% and portfolio loss at 6%
  8. Logging: Maintains complete audit trail of all trades for analysis
